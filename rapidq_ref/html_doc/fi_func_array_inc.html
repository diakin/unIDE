sub ArrAbsExtr (Dat!() as single, byref _AbsDatMax!)

cutPoints

SUB cutPoints (Dat!() as single, cutPnt as integer)

Shifts the contents of Dat!() left by cutPnt elements, dropping the first cutPnt points; the elements from index cutPnt to UBOUND-cutPnt-1 are copied to the start. The array is not resized, the tail keeps old values.

SUB OgibF (Dat1() AS SINGLE, OgibUp() AS SINGLE, OgibDn() AS SINGLE)

Builds upper and lower envelopes of Dat1(): local maxima (rise greater than 0.08) go to OgibUp() and local minima to OgibDn(), with straight-line interpolation between them. Both output arrays are REDIMed to UBOUND(Dat1).
