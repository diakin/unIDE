MinSet

SUB · rapidqAllProc.inc, line 10038

SUB MinSet (frmHandle as integer, appHandle as integer)

Makes a form minimize to the task bar as its own window: sets the owner (SetWindowLong index -8, GWL_HWNDPARENT) of the form to 0 and of the Application window to the form. Sample: MinSet(frmMain.Handle, Application.Handle).
