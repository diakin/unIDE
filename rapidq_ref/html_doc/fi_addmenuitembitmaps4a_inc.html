CreateMenuBitmap

SUB · RQProc.inc, line 6244

Sub CreateMenuBitmap (mPopup As QPopupMenu, mItem As Integer, flag_mItem As Long, Me As QBitmap)

Puts a bitmap (Me) on item mItem of popup menu mPopup, scaled to the menu check-mark size with transparency taken from pixel 0,0. flag_mItem selects by position (MF_BYPOSITION_AMIB) or by command. Uses CreateCompatibleDC, TransparentBlt and SetMenuItemBitmaps; shows a message on failure.
